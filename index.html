The problem

Requests stay buried in chats

91% of online adults in India message a business every week. The built-in way to organise those requests? Manual labels on whole chats.

These are real tasks — an order, a booking, a payment reminder. Today they just sit in the chat, scrolled past by morning.

How it works

Three steps, one slip

1

Paste or forward a message

Copy the customer's WhatsApp message exactly as it came — Hindi, English, Tamil, Hinglish, anything.

2

Pick your business type

Kirana, salon, clinic, tailor, tuition centre or other. This tells KaamQueue what kind of actions to extract.

3

Get your action slip

Request type, up to three actions with timing, a confirm checklist for anything you must verify, and a reply in the customer's language you can copy or send straight to WhatsApp. Every slip also lands in tomorrow's queue.

    Safety

    What KaamQueue never does

    🚫

    No price promises

    Never confirms prices, discounts, refunds or stock the owner hasn't verified.

    🔒

    No personal data stored

    Names, phone numbers and health details are stripped before anything is saved.

    💊

    No medical advice

    For clinic messages, it extracts the appointment request — never suggests diagnoses or treatments.

    🛡️

    Abuse-resistant

    Abusive or irrelevant messages are flagged, not processed. Reply tone stays courteous.
